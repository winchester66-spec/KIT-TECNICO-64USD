# DELIVERY, PAYMENT AND SUPPORT

## Delivery
- **Automatic and immediate:** as soon as the payment is approved by the checkout provider, an e-mail with the download links is sent to the address used at the order.
- **Lifetime access:** you can download the files again whenever you need them, on any computer.

## Payment
- Payments are processed in **USD** by our third-party checkout provider.
- Card data is handled entirely by the provider over an encrypted connection; we never see or store it.
- Refund terms are those displayed on the checkout page. See also `SITE.guaranteeDays` in `config.js`.

## Support
- Support covers download and access issues, extracting and preparing the USB drive, and using the toolkit.
- Contact: `SITE.supportEmail` in `config.js` (the whole site and the chat assistant read this single value).
