# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS — Tech Toolkit 64GB 2026

Short answers used on the landing page and by the chat assistant. The full machine-readable set lives in `knowledge/faq_*.json` and is merged into `knowledge/faq.json` by `chat/mergeFaq.js`.

### 1. How do I receive the toolkit after buying?
Delivery is 100% digital. Right after the payment is confirmed you receive an e-mail with the download links and access details.

### 2. Are the programs updated?
Yes. The built-in updater lists every application with its last update date and downloads the newest installer straight from the panel. Future updates are included at no extra cost.

### 3. Do I need internet to install drivers?
No. The offline driver pack covers chipset, network, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, audio and video — exactly the components that are missing right after a fresh Windows install.

### 4. What size USB drive do I need?
A 64 GB flash drive (USB 3.0 recommended) is enough for the complete package. An external SSD also works.

### 5. Which Windows versions are supported?
Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit) are the main targets. Many tools also work on Windows 7 machines.

### 6. Is there a machine limit?
No. Unlimited machines, unlimited clients, commercial use in your repair business included.

### 7. Does it include cracks, activators or pirated software?
No. Only legitimate installers for freely distributed, trial or vendor-supplied software, plus our own automation tools.

### 8. What language is the interface in?
The automation panel interface labels are currently in Portuguese. Program names, Windows and the installed software stay in your client's language.
